# Art Direction

Tone:
Institutional, global, credible, high-control, executive, modern logistics.

Avoid:
- Generic startup gradients
- Bubbly SaaS cards
- Overly playful copy
- Loud crypto-style neon styling

Use:
- Dark control-center aesthetic
- Deep slate and obsidian backgrounds
- Thin crisp borders
- Strong typography
- Quiet motion
- Status colors used semantically only

Reference feel:
A hybrid of premium finance dashboard, logistics control tower, and enterprise operations software.
